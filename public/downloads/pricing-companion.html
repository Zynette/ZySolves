The Pricing Companion

Understand the relationship between cost, selling price and gross margin.

How to use this tool

Enter all per-unit costs you want the price to cover. Choose a gross margin below 100%. This is margin, not markup.

Selling price = cost ÷ (1 − margin / 100). Gross profit = selling price − cost. Taxes, overhead and payment fees are excluded unless you include them in cost. A zero cost produces a zero model price; it does not determine the value of your time or work.
