Draft cancellation & refund policy

Draft for owner review · 26 November 2026. This preview is not accepting customer payments. Final business details and policies must be approved before launch.

Custom projects

Either party may request cancellation in writing. The proposed approach is to charge for completed, approved work and documented non-recoverable costs, then refund any unused balance. A deposit is not automatically forfeited regardless of work performed.

Studio cancellation

If the studio cannot complete the agreed work, unused funds are returned and usable paid-for deliverables are handed over as agreed. The project contract will confirm how completed milestones are valued.

Digital products

No paid products are currently available. Before a paid launch, each product must identify its files, compatibility, licence and support boundaries. Missing, corrupted or materially misdescribed files should be investigated and remedied through replacement, correction or an appropriate refund, subject to applicable rights.

Scheduled care

Proposed plans renew monthly. Cancel before the next renewal to avoid the next charge. Charges for completed work remain payable; any unused prepaid period is considered under the written agreement and applicable law.
