Draft service terms

Draft for owner review · 26 November 2026. This preview is not accepting customer payments. Final business details and policies must be approved before launch.

Scope comes first

Each project requires a written proposal covering deliverables, responsibilities, price, revision rounds and milestones. An inquiry does not confirm availability or create a project agreement. All displayed prices are starting prices in CAD.

Payments and timing

Proposed payment structure: 40% deposit after proposal acceptance and 60% before final transfer. Larger projects may use agreed milestones. Work begins once the deposit, required content and access are received. Client delays may move the schedule.

Revisions and scope changes

A revision round is one consolidated set of feedback on the agreed direction. New pages, new features or a changed direction require a written change quote. Extra work is not charged without approval.

Ownership and accounts

The proposal specifies which original deliverables transfer after full payment. Third-party materials and software retain their own licences. Clients are responsible for the rights to supplied content and should own their domain, hosting and platform accounts.

Ongoing costs and care

Hosting, domains, platform fees and subscriptions are excluded unless expressly included. Care plans cover scheduled work only, with time caps stated on the pricing page. They do not provide emergency availability or unlimited changes.

Invoices and disputes

Overdue work may be paused after written notice. No late fee is imposed by these draft terms. Concerns should first be raised with the studio so the parties can seek a practical resolution. Mandatory consumer rights are not waived.
