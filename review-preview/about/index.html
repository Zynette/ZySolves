ZySolves is a small, independent studio with a simple belief: useful things can be beautiful, and beautiful things should work well.

Ap.

ANTONETTE PETALLO · FOUNDER

Hello, I’m Antonette.

My background is in IT and business systems. I enjoy taking complicated details and making them clearer, whether that means a website that is easier to navigate or a workflow that gives each task a proper place.

ZySolves brings that technical foundation together with a love of expressive design. I work independently, so you have one point of contact from the first conversation to the final handover.

The studio is based in Canada and operates entirely online. Projects are shaped around a clear scope, thoughtful collaboration and realistic timelines.

Good things start with intention.

Begin with your idea.

Share your goals, priorities and where you are now. I’ll review the fit and discuss a clear scope with you.

Make room for the details.

You receive a written proposal with deliverables, milestones, revisions and pricing before work begins.

Bring it into the world.

We review, refine and test together. Your final handover includes the files and guidance agreed in your proposal.
