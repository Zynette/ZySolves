Privacy information

Draft for owner review · 26 November 2026. This preview is not accepting customer payments. Final business details and policies must be approved before launch.

Preview status

This is a review preview. Inquiry sending, payments and visitor analytics are disabled. Downloaded tools do not transmit your inputs to ZySolves. Your saved resource bag uses local browser storage. The Project Prelude saves a draft only when you choose Save.

When inquiries are enabled

The form will collect your name, email and the project details you choose to provide. These are used to review your inquiry and respond, not to subscribe you to marketing. Formspree will process submissions as our form provider; its processing and hosting arrangements must be reviewed before launch.

Retention and access

Proposed retention: delete unsuccessful inquiries after six months unless there is a continuing conversation or another legal reason to retain them. Contract and accounting records require a separate retention schedule. The owner must approve and implement this schedule before collecting inquiries.

Your choices

You can clear the saved bag through your browser’s site-data settings. Do not send confidential client records, passwords or payment data. The privacy contact address and procedures for access, correction and deletion will be confirmed before launch.

Hosting and analytics

Hosting providers may process standard request information for delivery and security. No advertising trackers or visitor analytics have been enabled in this build. Optional measurement must avoid inquiry content and require an updated disclosure.
