STUDIO NOTES

Before the first pixel.

Five things to gather before commissioning your website.

A useful website starts with a few clear decisions. You don’t need a perfect brand book or a finished business plan to begin. You do need enough context to make the design serve a purpose.

Start with one useful action

What should someone do after visiting? Ask for a quote, view your work, book through your existing platform or buy a product? Give the most important action a clear place.

Describe the person you want to reach

Write down what they need to know before trusting you. Include their practical questions: what you offer, what it costs, where you work and what happens next.

Gather what you already have

Collect your logo, colours, photographs and existing copy. Check that you have permission to use the images. Note which pieces need to be created as part of the project.

List the pages and connections

A list of five useful pages beats a list of twenty vague ideas. Also note existing tools such as your booking platform, form service or shop.

Agree on what finished means

Set deliverables, revision rounds, who supplies content and who owns the accounts. A clear handover is part of the design process.
