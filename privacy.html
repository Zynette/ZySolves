Privacy policy

Your data stays in your control

We use only the data needed to keep this site running and to respond to your inquiries. We do not sell your data.

What we collect

We collect only what is needed to run this site, improve it, and respond to your requests.

  • Analytics (optional): anonymous usage trends (page views, performance) to improve the experience.
  • Contact form details: name, email, and message so we can respond to your request.
  • Cookie consent: a simple yes/no flag stored in your browser so we don’t show the banner again.

How we use it

Your data helps us keep the site working and respond quickly when you reach out.

Improve the website

We look at broad trends (what pages are visited, what tools people click) to make the site easier to use.

Respond to inquiries

Contact form information is used only to reply to you and to coordinate your audit.

Keep things simple

We do not track individuals, build profiles, or resell your information.

Analytics (responsibly)

We use analytics tools to see how the site performs. We focus on trends, not individual visitors.

  • We may use a tool like Google Analytics or a privacy-friendly alternative.
  • We never combine analytics data with your contact form information.
  • If you would rather not be tracked, you can disable cookies in your browser.

Your choices

You can always control cookies and reach out to us if you have questions about your data.

  • Cookie banner: click “OK” to accept cookies, or clear your cookies to reset it.
  • Contact us: use the contact page or email us at hello@zysolves.com.
  • No sale of data: we do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing.
